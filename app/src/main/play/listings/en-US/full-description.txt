Chore Tracker helps couples and households keep chores fair, without the nagging.

Log a chore in one tap, see who has been carrying the load, take turns on the chores nobody wants, and ask each other for help when you need it.

• Quick logging: mark a chore done in one tap, for yourself, someone else or everyone together, for today, yesterday or any date, with an optional note.
• Fairness at a glance: Home shows how evenly the work was shared over the last 30 days.
• Take turns: let a chore rotate between you. Chore Tracker shows whose turn it is: whoever has done it least is up next, so covering for someone keeps them up until it evens out.
• Ask for help: ask someone to do a few chores, with an optional note. They get a notification and can mark them done or say they can't today. When a chore is due and it's someone else's turn, remind them in one tap.
• Chores that keep up: give each chore a category and a frequency, and overdue or due-soon chores are flagged automatically.
• Insights: each person's share of the work, the split by chore type, the last six months, and the history of every chore.
• Made for sharing: invite your partner or housemates with a code or a QR code. Make someone an admin to help run the household, and everyone can add chores and look after their own.
• Works offline: log chores without a connection, and changes sync to everyone when you're back online.
• Reminders that fit: an optional daily reminder for chores that need attention or that someone asked you to do. Turn-based chores remind only whoever's turn it is, and you can switch reminders off for single chores.
• English and Czech, light and dark theme.

Privacy: no ads and no usage analytics. Your household's data is visible only to its members, and you can delete your account and its data at any time in the app.
