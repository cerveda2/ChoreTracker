Chore Tracker

Privacy policy

Effective 1 November 2026

This policy explains what the Chore Tracker Android app ("the app") collects, why, and who can see it. Chore Tracker is a personal project by David Cervenka, Prague, Czech Republic, who is responsible for the data described here. Questions or requests: dadacervenka@gmail.com.

What the app collects and why

  • Your account. Your email address, display name and password, to let you sign in and to show your household who you are. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication and are never visible to us.
  • Your household's data. What you and the other members create: the household name, members (display name, email address, role), chores (name, category, how often), completions (who did what and when, plus an optional note) and invite codes. This is what the app syncs between the devices of your household.
  • A push notification token for your device, so the household owner can be notified when someone joins. You can turn this notification off in the app's settings, which also removes the token.
  • Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics): device model, Android version, app version, technical details of the crash and an installation identifier, so bugs can be found and fixed.
  • Usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase): automatically collected events such as app opens and sessions, device and app information, an app-instance identifier and an approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP address, to understand how the app is used.
  • The camera, only while you scan an invite QR code. The image is processed on your device and is never stored or uploaded.
  • On your device, the app keeps an offline copy of your household's data and your settings (theme, language, reminder time). Uninstalling the app removes them.

Who can see your data

The members of your household can see the household's data, including your display name, email address and the completions you log. Nobody outside your household can read it; this is enforced by the database's security rules.

The app shows no ads, and your data is never sold or shared with anyone else. It is stored and processed by Google as our service provider through Firebase (Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Functions, Cloud Messaging, Crashlytics and Google Analytics). Household data in Cloud Firestore and the app's Cloud Functions run in the European Union. Some Firebase services may process data outside the EU under Google's data protection terms. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

Legal basis (GDPR)

  • Your account, your household's data and the push token: needed to provide the app you signed up for (performance of a contract).
  • Crash reports and usage analytics: our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it.

How long data is kept

Account and household data is kept for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, it is removed as described on the account deletion page. Crash reports are kept by Crashlytics for 90 days. Analytics data is kept according to Google Analytics' retention settings.

Your rights

You can access, correct, export or delete your personal data, and object to its processing. You can change your display name and delete your account directly in the app; for anything else, email dadacervenka@gmail.com. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, in the Czech Republic the Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů.

Children

The app is intended for adults managing a household and is not directed at children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
