Delete your account

You can delete your Chore Tracker account and its data at any time, in the app or by email.

In the app

  1. Open Settings (Nastavení) and tap your profile at the top.
  2. On the Account (Účet) screen, scroll to Danger zone (Nebezpečná zóna).
  3. Tap Delete account (Smazat účet) and confirm.

The deletion happens immediately and you are signed out.

By email

If you no longer have the app, email dadacervenka@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my Chore Tracker account". Your account will be deleted within 30 days and you'll get a confirmation.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account and your user record, including your device's push notification token.
  • If you are the only member of your household: the whole household, with all its chores, completions and invites.
  • If other members have joined your household: your membership is deactivated, and if you were the owner, ownership passes to another member first. Your display name and email address stay on that inactive membership, so the household's history still shows who did what, and the remaining members keep the household with its chores and completions.

Crash reports and analytics data are not linked to your account. They expire on their own: Crashlytics keeps crash reports for 90 days, and analytics data is kept according to Google Analytics' retention settings. The offline copy on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app.
